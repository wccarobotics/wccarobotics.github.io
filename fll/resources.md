## Robotics Rules Videos

[Robotics Rules](https://www.roboticsrules.net/fll-c) publishes short, focused video tutorials for FLL Challenge teams, aimed especially at rookie and novice teams. Everything below is on their [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@RoboticsRules-DL).

### StarterBot

Their own base robot design, aimed squarely at rookie and novice teams, with free building instructions for the robot and a set of attachments.

- **[StarterBot — The AWESOME robot system for all novice and rookie FLL teams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZh5O4pW1y4)** (12:22) — Why the design works and how the attachment system fits together.
- **[StarterBot for Rookie and Novice FLL Teams — Live Build](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niSTzqKErSY)** (5:54) — A start-to-finish build.
- **[FLL StarterBot UPDATE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUt1MLcNirg)** (3:39) — Revisions to the design.
- **[Building instructions and attachment designs](https://www.roboticsrules.net/starter-bot)** — Free downloads for the StarterBot and eight attachments.

### Building

- **[Center of Gravity — The Key To Building an Effective FLL Robot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVthicvuX3w)** (10:43) — How center of gravity determines whether your robot tips or drives cleanly.
- **[Secure your FLL Robot to the Mission Structure Using a MASHER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xn6bF0jP1x4)** (4:52) — Locking the robot onto a mission model so the mission actually completes.
- **[AWESOME One-Way Door Designs for your FLL Robot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V96TLTVYCX4)** (6:48) — Passive designs for capturing mission elements.
- **[Who Needs Motors? Passive Attachment Designs for FLL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUbKb6AfTcU)** (8:16) — Turning a single button press into useful motion without an attachment motor.

Their channel also has coding tutorials, but those use the SPIKE Prime app rather than Pybricks — see [Programming](#programming) below for what our teams use.

## Programming

- **[M.A.R.C.U.S.](/marcus)** — Our own open-source SPIKE Prime menu framework for selecting and running mission programs during competition. Both of our FLL teams used it during the Unearthed season.
- **[Pybricks](https://pybricks.com/)** — The Python firmware our teams use with M.A.R.C.U.S. You can build programs with Pybricks block programming and convert them to Python, or write Python directly.
